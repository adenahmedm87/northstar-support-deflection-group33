# Team Charter — Northstar Support Deflection (Group 33)

## Communication
 Primary channel: WhatsApp group
 Daily check-in: everyone posts a 1-line status update by 8pm
 Expected response time: within 4 hours (9am–9pm)

## Deadlines
- Set on the board when a task is assigned, not after
- Flag a likely miss 12+ hours before it's due — no silent misses

## Conflict Resolution
1. Raise it in the team channel
2. Unresolved in 24h → team lead facilitates a call
3. Still unresolved → escalate to instructor/manager
